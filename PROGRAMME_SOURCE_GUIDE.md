# Programme Source Guide

## Official sources only

Programme facts must cite an official university page. Aggregator, ranking,
forum, social-media, and marketing pages are not accepted as factual sources.

Record the verification date and keep `refresh_required` true. The offline
fixture captures what was checked; it is not a live admissions database.

## Stable and volatile facts

Use only the smallest programme facts needed to explain portfolio fit. Course
identity and current curriculum themes may be recorded with an official source.

Exclude from generated material:

- deadlines;
- fees;
- contact names and email addresses;
- scholarship values and eligibility decisions;
- rankings;
- admission predictions.

These fields change too often or require applicant-specific interpretation.

## Refresh procedure

Before submission:

1. Open every official source URL.
2. Confirm the programme title and intended intake still exist.
3. Confirm any curriculum statement used in the draft.
4. Remove a fact that cannot be verified.
5. Update the verification date in a private working branch.
6. Rerun validation and review the complete rendered material.

The software does not scrape or refresh pages automatically.
