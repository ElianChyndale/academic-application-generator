# Human Review Checklist

Complete this checklist separately for every real application.

## Candidate facts

- [ ] Legal name and contact details match the application portal.
- [ ] Degree title, institution, dates, grades, and course names match official records.
- [ ] Employment titles and dates match verified employment records.
- [ ] Language-test details match the current score report.
- [ ] No private record has been committed to the public repository.

## Project evidence

- [ ] Every selected project claim still matches the tagged evidence dossier.
- [ ] Planned work is described only as future work.
- [ ] Synthetic and local-fixture results retain their limitations.
- [ ] Missing or blocked verification is not presented as success.
- [ ] PDF Manager remains EcoQuant support rather than a separate flagship.

## Programme facts

- [ ] The official programme page was rechecked immediately before submission.
- [ ] Programme title and curriculum wording are current.
- [ ] Professor name and research fit were verified from current faculty sources.
- [ ] Deadline, fee, scholarship, and document requirements were checked separately.
- [ ] No admission probability or ranking claim appears.

## Authorship and tone

- [ ] The candidate rewrote and approved the final narrative in their own voice.
- [ ] Every answer responds to the actual application prompt and word limit.
- [ ] No generated placeholder remains.
- [ ] A recommender independently authored any recommendation.
- [ ] The final document was proofread and exported from a private working copy.
