# Limitations

- The checked-in profile is intentionally incomplete and cannot produce a
  submission-ready CV or statement without authoritative private records.
- Generated prose is deterministic source material. The software does not
  assess semantic quality, authenticity, personal voice, prompt fit, or
  plagiarism.
- Programme facts are a dated snapshot and may change after the verification
  date.
- Official module records used for University of Limerick alignment do not
  replace a current programme page or applicant-specific admissions guidance.
- Project claims are inherited from a frozen dossier release; they do not
  continuously monitor source repositories.
- Claim citation proves declared provenance, not that a paraphrase is the best
  possible interpretation for every audience.
- The validator detects selected contradiction and overstatement patterns; it
  cannot prove that all subtle narrative inconsistencies are absent.
- No application, email, scholarship request, or recommendation is submitted or
  sent by this project.
- No admission or scholarship outcome can be inferred from generated material.
- Final authorship and factual responsibility remain with the candidate.
