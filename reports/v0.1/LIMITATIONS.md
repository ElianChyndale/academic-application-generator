# Release limitations

- Generated text is deterministic source material, not a finished application.
- No semantic quality, authenticity, admission, or scholarship outcome is scored.
- Official programme facts were checked on the release date and can change.
- No grades, transcript courses, employment history, awards, publications, language results, or recommendation statements are present.
- Project evidence comes from a frozen tagged dossier release and does not continuously monitor source repositories.
- Every draft requires candidate review and programme-specific fact refresh.
