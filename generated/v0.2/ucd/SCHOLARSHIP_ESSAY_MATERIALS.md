## Evidence of preparation

The public portfolio demonstrates Python, TypeScript, Solidity, retrieval evaluation, risk simulation, technical writing, and reproducibility practices. A bounded PDF Manager support flow creates structured evidence from a synthetic document and passed its targeted test. The valuation script produces deterministic spread and price sensitivity scenarios and passed the targeted research test layer. The repository implements independent local runners across core AI method areas and writes outputs under a shared research directory. I designed a multi-experiment research programme (E0 through E8) around one central question: how retrieval, table reasoning, temporal contradiction handling, verification, calibration, and human oversight combine for reliable financial document intelligence. On the FinanceBench public sample of real 10-K questions, dense retrieval achieved higher Recall-at-K than sparse and long-context baselines, with company-clustered bootstrap confidence intervals; method preference reversed across datasets. Retrieval confidence separated correct from incorrect predictions with high AUROC; at high supported-answer precision, only a small fraction of cases could be auto-accepted — calibration certifies precision but does not create it. This material documents preparation and intended contribution; it does not assert a scholarship outcome.
